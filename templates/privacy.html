01. Overview & Commitment

AutoRide Sniper ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of auto-rickshaw, cab, bike-taxi, and delivery partners across India. We believe in 100% transparency regarding what permissions our Android application requires and why.

🛡️ Our Core Guarantee: We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information. We do not inspect personal WhatsApp chats, banking apps, or passwords.

02. Android Accessibility Service Disclosure

AutoRide Sniper relies on the standard Android AccessibilityService API to provide core automated assistance for drivers on the road. In strict compliance with Android developer policies:

  • Offer Screen Inspection: The service reads only active ride offer overlay windows (fare amount, pickup distance, and platform name) from authorized partner apps (e.g., Ola Driver, Uber Driver, Rapido Captain, inDrive, Porter).
  • Automated Micro-Taps: When an incoming offer matches your pre-set Minimum Fare and Maximum Distance filters, the service simulates a standard finger tap on the platform's "Accept" button.
  • Zero Sensitive Access: The service CANNOT and DOES NOT monitor keystrokes, personal messages, payment credentials, OTPs, or passwords.

03. Information We Collect

  • Driver Account Details: Mobile phone number, driver name, vehicle number, city, and preferred language (Marathi, Hindi, English).
  • Hardware Device Binding: A unique cryptographic hardware identifier (Android DRM/Device ID) to prevent license piracy and enforce one active phone per license.
  • Telemetry & Diagnostics: App version, operating status (Engine Active / Idle), and ride match metrics to help resolve sync issues and platform updates.

04. Data Security & Retention

All communication between your phone and our cloud infrastructure is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols. Passwords are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 salts. We retain subscription records for the duration of your active license.

05. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to request full deletion of your driver profile, hardware binding unbinds, and ride logs at any time. To request data deletion or license transfer, contact us via Telegram or WhatsApp helpline.
